Get to know Motionly

What can I create with Motionly?

Motion ads, product launches, feature announcements, demos, and short-form videos built around your real product.

How does it start?

Paste your product link or describe your brand and launch moment. Motionly uses that context to shape the first ad.

Can I change the result?

Yes. Tell Motionly what to change — the hook, pacing, scene, voiceover, or visual direction — and keep refining by chat.

Do I need motion-design experience?

No. Start with a sentence, choose a direction, and make adjustments in plain language.

What does the finished ad include?

A polished motion composition built around your product, with scenes, pacing, and visual direction ready to refine.

Can I use the videos for paid ads?

Yes. Create launch videos and variations for social posts, paid campaigns, product pages, and demos.

Which formats can I make?

Shape your output for the channels where your audience already is, from vertical short-form video to wider product demos.

Do I need a card to try Motionly?

No. Start free, explore the workflow, and see the first direction before committing to anything.
