Why We Built a New Motion Format

We wanted a workflow where you can generate drafts with AI, edit them visually on a timeline, and scale them with code—without losing editability.

01

High barrier to create

Creating custom motion graphics usually requires specialized tools and skills, making it difficult for small teams to produce polished animations.

02

Large files slow AI iteration

Code-generated animations can become difficult for AI to understand and modify, increasing the cost of making even small changes.

03

Hard to scale video production

Creating more animations often means repeating the same manual process, making it difficult to produce consistent, reusable content at scale.

"I spent hours trying to find inspiration, specific templates to get started on a promotion video on our new feature. Some animations are so specific, I can't find it on the internet. So I started Motionly, to hopefully solve that."
